Echo is here — the new middle tab. 🎧

It turns your conversations into review cards. Hold any message, yours or theirs, and choose Add to Echo: the sentence becomes a card, with the meaning in your own language filled in for you. The same tap is at the end of a translation and on a post in the Feed, and on the cards screen you can write one yourself with +.

Then Echo brings it back — tomorrow, in three days, in a week, in a month — each time just before you would have forgotten it. Remember it and the next wait gets longer; forget it and it comes back sooner. Nothing is lost.

Nothing kept yet? Start from one of the ready-made packs. Echo is free on every plan.

Ten minutes a day beats an hour once a week — and these are your own sentences, out of your own chats. 💛
