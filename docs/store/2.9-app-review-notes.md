The text for **App Store Connect → the 2.9 version → App Review Information →
Notes**. 2.9 is the first build that rings a phone: voice and video calls
(#1711) and the chat camera with view-once photos and videos (#1705, #1713).
The note tells the reviewer how to reach both, and answers the two questions
App Review asks about calling apps before they are asked — what PushKit VoIP is
used for, and what happens to the media.

fields stay as they are. No password belongs in this file or anywhere else in

## The note

Apple's limit for this field is 4,000 characters; the text below is under it.

```text
LangX 2.9 adds voice and video calls between people who chat, and a chat camera that can send a photo or video as "View once".

DEMO ACCOUNTS
- Account A: the account in Sign-In Information above (@bob_reviewer).
- Account B: @langx_review — [sign-in for Account B, added in App Store Connect only]
The two accounts already share a conversation in which each has sent the other more than 5 messages, so calls and the camera are unlocked there. Please use that conversation for testing.

HOW TO TRY A CALL
1. Sign in as Account A on the review device, and as Account B on a second device or in a desktop browser at https://app.langx.io (Chrome or Safari; allow microphone and camera).
2. On either side, open Chats and the conversation with the other account.
3. Tap the phone button at the top right of the conversation and choose Voice call or Video call.
4. The other side rings. On an iPhone or iPad the incoming call uses the system call screen (CallKit), also when the app is closed or the device is locked. Answer, talk, hang up. A row for the call appears in the conversation.

WHO CAN CALL WHOM
- You can call someone only after they have sent you 5 messages in that conversation (the same rule that unlocks photos). Before that the app explains how many messages are still needed. This keeps strangers from making someone's phone ring.
- Settings > Privacy > "Allow calls" turns calls off in both directions: with it off, nobody can call you and you cannot call anyone.
- Official accounts (such as @langx) cannot call or be called.
- After three unanswered calls in a row, the caller has to wait until the other person writes or calls back.

PUSHKIT VOIP AND CALLKIT
PushKit VoIP pushes are used only to ring an incoming call. Every VoIP push is reported to CallKit as an incoming call as soon as it arrives (a malformed one is reported and ended at once). Messages and every other notification use regular push notifications, never VoIP. On the China storefront CallKit and PushKit are not used; an incoming call there arrives as a regular notification.

PRIVACY OF CALLS
Calls are WebRTC between the two devices and encrypted end to end by the protocol. Media always goes through a TURN relay, so neither person learns the other's IP address. Calls are never recorded and we have no media server; our server keeps only who called whom, when, and how the call ended, for 30 days.

CHAT CAMERA AND VIEW ONCE
In the same conversation, tap the camera button next to the message field: tap for a photo, hold for a video. Before sending choose "View once", "Allow replay" (opened once, replayed once) or "Keep in chat". While a view-once photo or video is open the app blocks screenshots and screen recording, and tells the sender if one was attempted.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
LangX Pro is an auto-renewable subscription. The Plans screen is at Me > Settings (gear) > Subscription > See the plans. The Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA), Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy are linked at the end of the App Store description and on the Plans screen. Nothing about subscriptions changed in this version.
```

## What the note leans on

Each claim in it is the code's, so a reviewer who tests it finds it true:

- **Every VoIP push reports a call** — `modules/calls/ios/CallCenter.swift`
  (`reportIncoming`: a payload without a usable call id is still reported, then
  ended as failed). One case returns without a new report: a second push for
  the call that is already on screen.
- **No VoIP push for anything else** — `docs/decisions.md` → _Calls_: the API
  sends a VoIP push only to ring.
- **China** — `CallCenter.swift` checks the storefront for `CHN` and never
  starts PushKit there (`docs/release-runbook.md` → _Calling is off until it is
  given a relay_).
- **Relay only, no recording, 30 days** — `docs/decisions.md` → _Calls: two
  devices, a relay, and a server that only keeps the state_.
- **The 5-message rule, Allow calls, official accounts, three unanswered
  calls** — `apps/api/src/modules/calls/access.ts`.
- **The camera behind the same lock** — `ChatScreen.tsx` `openCamera()`.
- **View once** — `docs/decisions.md` → _View-once hides, it does not delete_.
- **Subscriptions and the EULA link** — `docs/store/listing.md`; the two earlier
  rejections (2.1(b) and 3.1.2) are why the last paragraph is there.
