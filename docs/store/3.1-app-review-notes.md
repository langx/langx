# App Review notes — 3.1 (draft)

The text for **App Store Connect → the 3.1 version → App Review Information →
Notes**. A draft until the 3.1 build is submitted; replace "draft" with the
date it was entered.

It assumes the review switches are: `whatsappStickers` **on** (otherwise "Add
to WhatsApp" never draws and the paragraph about it describes nothing the
reviewer can open — Guideline 2.3.1), and the rest as for 3.0 (`proWeek`,
`giveAWeek`, `newcomerHello`, `stickerGifts` off).

**China mainland goes back into Pricing and Availability in the same
submission.** It was taken out on 13 October 2026 after 2.9 was rejected twice
under Guideline 5 ("CallKit must not be active on the China App Store"). The
second rejection came after a reply that said what the code already did, so
this note says what changed in the build, first, and how the reviewer can see
it. Re-adding China without the build would invite the same rejection.

What the CHINA paragraph rests on is held by
`apps/mobile/src/lib/calls/callKitChina.test.ts`: if an edit makes any of it
untrue, CI fails.

```text
LangX 3.1 adds Venus stickers for WhatsApp and filters on recorded videos, and turns CallKit off on the China storefront. Permissions, background modes and subscriptions are as in 3.0 (build 198).

CHINA STOREFRONT: NO CALLKIT
We have added China mainland back to availability with this build. On the China storefront this build creates no CallKit object at all:
- At launch the app decides once whether CallKit may be used: never when the App Store storefront is China (CHN), never when the device region is China (CN), and never when the storefront is not yet known. The decision does not change while the app runs.
- When the answer is no, PushKit is never started (no VoIP token is registered, so no VoIP push can be sent to the device), CXProvider and CXCallController are never created, and the Siri intent "Call <name> on LangX" (INStartCallIntent) is refused.
- Incoming calls then ring through an ordinary push notification, and the call is answered and shown on LangX's own in-app call screen. There is no system call screen, no call entry in Recents, and no CarPlay or Siri calling.
Outside China, calls work as in 3.0.

DEMO ACCOUNTS
Account A is in Sign-In Information above (@langx_review); Account B is @langx_review2. Each has sent the other more than 5 messages, so calls and the chat camera are unlocked in their conversation.

WHAT CHANGED
- Venus on WhatsApp: in the conversation with Account B, tap the sticker button beside the text field, then the arrow (>) beside the name "Venus" (a free sticker pack) to open its page, then "Add to WhatsApp". This opens WhatsApp, which asks before adding the pack of 30 stickers. The button shows only when WhatsApp is installed.
- Video filters: in the conversation with Account B, tap the camera button, hold to record a video, swipe left or right on the viewfinder to choose a filter, then send. The filter is applied to the video on the device before it is sent.
- Photos and videos sent from the chat camera now default to "Allow replay" (the other person can open it one more time). "View once" and "Keep in chat" are still there. While a view-once item is open the app blocks screenshots and screen recording.
- Saving a photo or video from a chat now dates it at the moment of saving, so it appears at the end of the Photos library. The app still asks only for add-only photo library access.

COPILOT (BETA, UNCHANGED SINCE 3.0)
An AI helper inside a chat. With Account B: type a sentence and tap the Copilot button beside send ("Check my sentence", "Say it in [language]"); long-press their message for "Explain"; an empty draft after their newest message shows "Reply ideas".
First use opens a consent sheet naming the provider (Anthropic), what is sent (the text asked about, with your languages and levels; for Reply ideas the last few messages of that chat) and what never is (name, email, photos, voice notes). Nothing is sent before you accept. Answers are labelled "Copilot · Beta", reach the other person only if you send them, and have "Report". Settings > Privacy > Copilot withdraws consent.

CALLS (OUTSIDE CHINA, UNCHANGED SINCE 3.0)
In the chat between the two accounts, tap the phone button (top right), then Voice or Video call. Incoming calls use the system call screen (CallKit), also when the app is closed. You can call someone only after they have sent you 5 messages; Settings > Privacy > "Allow calls" turns calls off both ways.
VoIP pushes only ring incoming calls, each reported to CallKit on arrival; the "voip" and "audio" background modes are for calls only. Siri and CarPlay: "Call <name> on LangX". Calls are WebRTC, never recorded.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
LangX Pro, auto-renewable, monthly or yearly, with a one-week free trial where the store offers it: Me > Settings (gear) > Subscription > See the plans. The Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA), Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy are linked there and at the end of the App Store description.
```

Before entering it, check against the build being submitted:

- the path to the Venus pack (sticker button, then the arrow beside "Venus")
  on the build itself;
- `copilotEnabled` is on (the COPILOT paragraph assumes it, as since
  25 October 2026); drop the paragraph if it is not;
- the "Allow replay" default (`DEFAULT_SNAP_MODE`) is still `'replay'`;
- the block still fits App Store Connect's 4000-character limit for Notes
  (3,939 on 26 October 2026). The first draft was 4,345 and would have been
  refused, which is why the paragraphs about what 3.0 already had are terse.
