# App Review notes — 3.2

The text for **App Store Connect → the 3.2 version → App Review Information →
Notes**, entered on 2 November 2026 with the 3.2 submission. It is 3.1's
approved note with the opener and WHAT CHANGED rewritten; CHINA, DEMO
ACCOUNTS, COPILOT, CALLS and SUBSCRIPTIONS are 3.1's word for word, apart from
China now being described as staying available rather than coming back.

The review switches are as for 3.1: `whatsappStickers` and `copilotEnabled`
**on**, `proWeek`, `giveAWeek`, `newcomerHello` and `stickerGifts` off.

3.2 is also the first Android build without the Android Auto declaration (Play
rejected 2.9 on the car-quality review, 2 November 2026). Nothing about that
reaches this note: CarPlay is a separate, iOS-side feature and is unchanged.

```text
LangX 3.2 adds moderator applications and a clear notice in chats with deleted accounts. Permissions, background modes, subscriptions and the China storefront handling are unchanged from 3.1.

CHINA STOREFRONT: NO CALLKIT
China mainland stays available, as for 3.1 (approved 2 Nov). On the China storefront this build creates no CallKit object at all:
- At launch the app decides once whether CallKit may be used: never when the App Store storefront is China (CHN), never when the device region is China (CN), and never when the storefront is not yet known. The decision does not change while the app runs.
- When the answer is no, PushKit is never started (no VoIP token is registered, so no VoIP push can be sent to the device), CXProvider and CXCallController are never created, and the Siri intent "Call <name> on LangX" (INStartCallIntent) is refused.
- Incoming calls then ring through an ordinary push notification, and the call is answered and shown on LangX's own in-app call screen. There is no system call screen, no call entry in Recents, and no CarPlay or Siri calling.
Outside China, calls work as in 3.0 and 3.1.

DEMO ACCOUNTS
Account A is in Sign-In Information above (@langx_review); Account B is @langx_review2. Each has sent the other more than 5 messages, so calls and the chat camera are unlocked in their conversation.

WHAT CHANGED
- Moderators: a profile of one of our moderators shows a MOD badge; tapping it opens "What’s a moderator?", which says what moderators do and that our team reviews every decision. Its "Want to become a mod?" button needs a 60-day streak, so on the demo accounts it is disabled and shows the days left. An application is four written answers that go to our team’s queue; appointing a moderator is done by hand.
- A conversation with a deleted account now says the account was deleted and has no text field, so nothing can be sent to it.
- On the LangX Token page, the "Invite bonus" row opens the invite page.
From 3.1, unchanged: Venus stickers for WhatsApp, filters on recorded videos, "Allow replay" as the camera default, and the Venus, Copilot, LangX Token and Transparency pages behind Home’s four cards.

COPILOT (BETA, UNCHANGED SINCE 3.0)
With Account B: type a sentence and tap the Copilot button beside send; long-press their message for "Explain"; an empty draft after their newest message shows "Reply ideas".
First use opens a consent sheet naming the provider (Anthropic), what is sent and what never is. Nothing is sent before you accept. Answers are labelled "Copilot · Beta", reach the other person only if you send them, and have "Report". Settings > Privacy > Copilot withdraws consent.

CALLS (OUTSIDE CHINA, UNCHANGED SINCE 3.0)
With Account B, tap the phone button (top right), then Voice or Video call. Incoming calls use CallKit's call screen, also when the app is closed. Settings > Privacy > "Allow calls" turns calls off both ways.
VoIP pushes only ring incoming calls, each reported to CallKit on arrival; the "voip" and "audio" background modes are for calls only. Siri and CarPlay: "Call <name> on LangX". Calls are WebRTC, never recorded.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
LangX Pro, auto-renewable, monthly or yearly, with a one-week free trial where offered: Me > Settings (gear) > Subscription > See the plans. The Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA), Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy are linked there and at the end of the App Store description.
```
