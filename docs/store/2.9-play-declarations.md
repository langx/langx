# Google Play declarations — 2.9

What to enter in Play Console for the 2.9 bundle, written to be pasted. 2.9 is
the calls release (#1711) and the release with the chat camera (#1705, #1713),
and both reach Android with permissions Play asks about. The reasoning behind
each answer is in `docs/decisions.md` → _Calls: two devices, a relay, and a
server that only keeps the state_ and → _View-once hides, it does not delete_.

`CallService` is declared with `foregroundServiceType="phoneCall|microphone|camera"`
and starts with exactly the types the moment needs: `phoneCall` while ringing;
`phoneCall | microphone` for a voice call in progress; `phoneCall | microphone |
camera` for a video call in progress. It stops when the call ends.

## Foreground service permissions

_App content → Foreground service permissions._ One entry per type. Where the
form offers a list of tasks, tick the one named; paste the description; one
video covers all three (see below).

### `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_PHONE_CALL`

- **Task:** Phone call / ongoing call (VoIP).
- **Description:**

```text
LangX lets two people who are practising a language together make voice and video calls to each other inside the app. The phoneCall foreground service runs only for a call: while an incoming call is ringing (shown as a CallStyle notification with Answer and Decline, and as a full-screen call screen on a locked device), and while a call the user started or answered is in progress (an ongoing CallStyle notification with Hang up). It is started by a call ringing or being answered and stops as soon as the call is declined, missed, or ended by either person. It never runs at any other time.
```

- **Impact if deferred or interrupted:**

```text
The call would be lost. An incoming call that is not shown at once is a missed call (it rings for at most 45 seconds), and a call in progress that is stopped is cut off for both people mid-conversation. The service is what keeps the call alive while the user switches apps or locks the screen.
```

### `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE`

- **Task:** Voice or video call (communication).
- **Description:**

```text
During a voice or video call the user has answered or placed, LangX keeps capturing the microphone so the other person can still hear them when the user switches to another app or the screen turns off. The microphone type is added only after the call connects and is dropped when the call ends; it is never used while ringing or outside a call. The user sees an ongoing call notification with a Hang up button for the whole time.
```

- **Impact if deferred or interrupted:**

```text
The other person would stop hearing the user as soon as the user left the app or the screen locked, and the call would continue in silence. The user would have to stay inside the app with the screen on for the whole call.
```

### `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CAMERA`

- **Task:** Video call (communication).
- **Description:**

```text
During a video call the user has answered or placed with their camera on, LangX keeps the camera stream going while the user briefly leaves the app, so the call does not freeze or drop for the other person. The camera type is added only when a video call connects and is removed when the call ends; a voice call never uses it. The ongoing call notification with Hang up is visible the whole time, and the user can turn the camera off from the call screen at any moment.
```

- **Impact if deferred or interrupted:**

```text
The other person's view of the user would freeze or the video call would fail whenever the user switched apps, for example to look something up during the conversation.
```

### The video Play asks for

One screen recording, about one to two minutes, on an Android phone with the
2.9 build, using two demo accounts that already share an unlocked conversation
(each has sent the other at least 5 messages). No real users, no personal data
on screen. Upload it unlisted (YouTube) or as a shared link, and give the same
link for all three types. It must show, in order:

1. **Ringing with the app closed** — the app swiped away and the phone locked;
   the other account calls; the full-screen incoming call appears on the lock
   screen. Then, unlocked, a second call arriving as the heads-up CallStyle
   notification with Answer and Decline.
2. **A voice call kept alive in the background** — answer; talk; press Home;
   the ongoing call notification with Hang up stays in the shade and the audio
   continues (`phoneCall` + `microphone`).
3. **A video call kept alive in the background** — start a video call from the
   phone button at the top of the conversation; both cameras on; switch to
   another app and back; the video continues (`camera`).
4. **The service ending with the call** — hang up from the notification; the
   notification disappears.

## Full-screen intent

_App content → Full-screen intent (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT)._

- **Core functionality:** Calling — the app receives voice and video calls.
- **Justification:**

```text
LangX includes one-to-one voice and video calling between people who practise a language together. An incoming call uses a full-screen intent only while it rings, so the person can see who is calling and answer or decline from the lock screen, exactly like a phone call. The full-screen intent is attached to the incoming-call notification (CallStyle) and to nothing else: no message, reminder, promotion or other notification uses it. When the device is unlocked and in use, the call appears as a heads-up notification instead.
```

## Data safety — what changes

Read from the public Data safety page on 6 October 2026: Messages (other in-app
messages), App activity (app interactions, other user-generated content), Audio
(voice or sound recordings), Device or other IDs, Personal info (name, email,
user IDs, other info), Location (approximate), **Photos and videos (Photos
only)**; nothing shared; encrypted in transit; deletion on request.

- [ ] **Photos and videos → Videos: add.** Collected, not shared, optional,
      purpose App functionality, not processed ephemerally. The chat camera
      records video clips ("hold for video"), and videos sent in a chat are
      stored like photos (`messages/<conversationId>/` in the bucket). The form
      today declares Photos only. This is the one real delta.
- [ ] **Call audio and video: no new answer.** The media of a call is WebRTC
      between the two devices, end-to-end encrypted by the protocol, and passes
      through the TURN relay as ciphertext only. Nothing of it reaches our
      servers, nothing is recorded, and nobody but the two people can read it.
      Play does not count end-to-end encrypted data as collected — check the
      help text the form shows before relying on that sentence. Audio stays
      declared for voice messages, as it is today.
- [ ] **The call record: no new answer.** What the server keeps — who called
      whom, when, from which device, and how it ended, deleted after 30 days,
      plus the call's row in the conversation — is part of the conversation,
      already declared as Messages → Other in-app messages and App activity →
      Other user-generated content.
- [ ] **Microphone and camera access: no new data type.** Play's form asks
      about data, not permissions; what leaves the phone is covered above.
- [ ] **Security practices: unchanged** — encrypted in transit stays yes.

## Photo and video permissions

The existing declaration (3 September 2026) names `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, a
leftover of v1's bundles. 2.9 declares neither `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` nor
`READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`: `expo-screen-capture` brings `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` and
`app.config.ts` blocks it, and `expo-media-library` runs with
`granularPermissions: []`. Nothing to file for 2.9. If Play asks again once the
bundle is uploaded, the answer is that the app does not request broad photo or
video access: picking uses the system photo picker, and saving to the gallery
needs no permission on Android 11+.
