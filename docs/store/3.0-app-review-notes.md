# App Review notes — 3.0

The text in **App Store Connect → the 3.0 version → App Review Information →
Notes**, as entered on 23 Oct 2026. It describes only what the reviewer can
reach with the switches as they are for the review: `v3Home` on; `proWeek`,
`giveAWeek`, `newcomerHello` and `stickerGifts` off, so the Pro Week, give a
week get a week, the newcomer hello and sticker pack gifts are left out. Each
one goes into a later submission's note when its switch goes on (Guideline
2.3.1: never describe a feature the reviewer cannot open).

Copilot is described for Account A. Since 25 October 2026 every account has
it while `copilotEnabled` is on, so nothing needs setting for the review.

The earlier draft, which described the switched-off features, is in git history.

```text
LangX 3.0 is a redesign of the whole app. Permissions, background modes and subscriptions are the same as in 2.9 (approved as build 191).

DEMO ACCOUNTS
- Account A: the account in Sign-In Information above (@langx_review).
- Account B: @langx_review2. The two share a conversation in which each has sent the other more than 5 messages, so calls and the chat camera are unlocked there.

WHAT CHANGED
- New look and navigation: Home, Chats, Echo, People, Me. Home opens on the next thing to do: who is waiting for a reply, the day's streak, the Echo review cards that are ready.
- Chat camera: filters before sending. Calls and the chat camera work as in 2.9, on new screens.
- 17 app icons to choose from (Me > Settings > App icon); nine of them need LangX Pro. New badges on profiles.
- Sticker packs and profile frames are bought with tokens. Tokens are in-app points earned by using the app (talking, correcting, reviewing). They cannot be bought with money, sold, transferred or withdrawn.

COPILOT (BETA, FOR CHOSEN ACCOUNTS)
Copilot is an AI helper inside a chat, on for accounts we choose; Account A has it. In the conversation with Account B, type a sentence, then tap the Copilot button beside the send button for "Check my sentence" or "Say it in [language]"; long-press one of Account B's messages for "Explain"; when the other person's newest message is unanswered and your draft is empty, a "Reply ideas" chip appears above it.
The first use opens a consent sheet that names the provider (Anthropic), says what is sent (the text asked about, with your languages and levels; for Reply ideas the last few messages of that chat) and what is never sent (name, email, photos, voice notes). Nothing reaches the provider before you accept. Answers are labelled "Copilot · Beta", and nothing is sent to the other person unless you send it yourself. Every answer has "Report". Settings > Privacy > Copilot withdraws consent.

CALLS (UNCHANGED SINCE 2.9)
Open the conversation between the two accounts, tap the phone button at the top right and choose Voice call or Video call. On an iPhone or iPad the incoming call uses the system call screen (CallKit), also when the app is closed. You can call someone only after they have sent you 5 messages; Settings > Privacy > "Allow calls" turns calls off both ways. Screen recording of the full flow: https://youtube.com/shorts/tdoKsisGK80
PushKit VoIP pushes are used only to ring an incoming call, and each is reported to CallKit as soon as it arrives. The "voip" and "audio" background modes are for calls only. On the China storefront CallKit and PushKit are not used. "Call <name> on LangX" works with Siri and in CarPlay. Calls are WebRTC through a TURN relay, never recorded.

VIEW ONCE
In a conversation, tap the camera button: tap for a photo, hold for a video, then choose "View once", "Allow replay" or "Keep in chat". While a view-once item is open the app blocks screenshots and screen recording.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
LangX Pro is an auto-renewable subscription, monthly or yearly, with a one-week free trial where the store offers it. The Plans screen is at Me > Settings (gear) > Subscription > See the plans. The Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA), Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy are linked at the end of the App Store description and on the Plans screen.
```
