## The paragraph

Under Apple's 4,000-character limit together with the rest of a normal note.

```text
STICKER PACK GIFTS
LangX has an in-app point called tokens. Tokens come from activity in the app (for example sending messages, correcting someone's sentence, recording a pronunciation, finishing a review, keeping a streak) and from small bonuses LangX gives, such as a starting balance and a free hourly gift. They cannot be purchased with money or anything else, have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn, and cannot be sent from one user to another. They are spent only on streak freezes, missed-day repairs and cosmetic items such as sticker packs. They cannot buy the subscription or any of its features.

A user can spend their own earned tokens to give a sticker pack to someone they chat with. The giver pays for a new copy of the pack, and the pack becomes the recipient's. No tokens move between the two users: the recipient's balance does not change. A gift cannot be passed on, returned or refunded. A gift carries no message of its own; it appears in the conversation as a card showing the pack.

Limits:
- Both people must be 18 or older. Users under 18 can neither give nor receive a gift.
- Only between people in an established conversation: each has sent the other at least 50 messages, and the conversation is at least 3 days old. The giver's account must be at least 14 days old.
- One gift sent per week, two received per month.
- No gifts where either person has blocked or reported the other, to or from suspended accounts, or to official accounts.
- Only sticker packs can be given. Frames, titles and streak items cannot.

TO TRY IT
Sign in as Account A and open the conversation with Account B [sign-in for Account B, added in App Store Connect only]. Tap the sticker button next to the message field, open a sticker pack, and tap "Give this pack". Pick Account B, then confirm. The pack appears in Account B's stickers, and a card for the gift appears in the conversation for both.
```

## What the paragraph leans on

Each claim is the code's or the Terms', so a reviewer who tests it finds it
true:

- **Activity and bonuses, never bought.** `packages/shared/src/token.ts`
  (the header comment and `TOKEN_KINDS`): besides activity, the starting
  balance, the hourly gift, the daily pool, referrals and the operator's
  bounty and report rewards. No kind is credited by a payment from the person
  who receives it. **One exception to watch:** `referralSubscription` pays the
  _referrer_ when somebody they invited starts a paid plan. Nobody can buy
  their own tokens that way, and `TOKEN_RULES.referral.subscription` says why
  that is not a purchase. Still, a reviewer who asks "can money ever produce
  tokens" deserves that answer, not "never". The note says "cannot be
  purchased", which is true of every user.
- **Spent only on freezes, repairs and cosmetics, never the subscription.**
  `TOKEN_RULES.sinks`, the `token.ts` header, `docs/decisions.md`. The note
  says "the subscription or any of its features" rather than "no paid
  feature" on purpose: a freeze protects a streak, and a streak can earn a
  gift of Pro, which is the same reason streak items cannot be given.
- **A new copy, no tokens moved, the recipient's balance unchanged.**
  `apps/api/src/modules/tokens/stickerGifts.ts`: one transaction charges the
  giver and grants the pack. Before they confirm, the giver reads
  `stickerGift.honesty`: the pack becomes theirs, cannot be returned or
  refunded, and the tokens are not given back.
- **Cannot be passed on, returned or refunded.** Terms §7, _Gifts between
  users_ (langx/website#288).
- **18 and over at both ends.** `isAdult` on both sides in `stickerGifts.ts`;
  the Terms §7 say the same.
- **50 messages each, 3 days, 14-day giver, 1 a week, 2 a month.**
  `STICKER_GIFT_RULES` in `packages/shared/src/stickerGift.ts`. The caps are
  unique indexes in `apps/api/src/db/indexes.ts`, not counts.
- **Blocks, reports, suspended, official.** The refusals listed in
  `docs/decisions.md` → _A sticker pack can be given to a chat partner, behind
  a switch_.
- **No message with a gift.** There is no free-text field. The row is written
  by the server (`isServerRecord`).
- **Sticker packs only.** `isGiftableCosmetic`.

**Why it holds under 3.1.1.** In-app purchase rules apply to digital items
bought with money. Nothing here is bought with money: the tokens are earned,
and they are not for sale. If tokens or items are ever sold, this paragraph
stops being true and gifting falls under in-app purchase rules. The gifting
entry in `docs/decisions.md` says so too.
