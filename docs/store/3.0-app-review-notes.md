# App Review notes — 3.0 (draft)

The text for **App Store Connect → the 3.0 version → App Review Information →
Notes**. 3.0 is the redesign release: every screen in the v3 look, and the
features built behind switches for it — the Pro Week, give a week get a week,
the newcomer hello, Copilot as a beta for chosen accounts, seven new sticker
packs, new badges and 17 app icons. Calls and the chat camera are 2.9's,
redrawn.

A draft, English only. Behic pastes it himself; nothing here has been sent to
App Store Connect. The **Sign-In Information** fields (demo username and
password) and the contact fields stay as they are. No password belongs in this
file or anywhere else in the repo: the second account's sign-in goes into the
note in App Store Connect itself, in place of the bracketed line.

## The note

Apple's limit for this field is 4,000 characters; the text below is under it.
The calls privacy paragraph from 2.9 (WebRTC, relay only, never recorded) is
left out to fit; 2.9 was approved with it and nothing about it changed.

```text
LangX 3.0 is a redesign of the whole app, plus a few features that came with it. Permissions and background modes are the same as in 2.9. The one subscription change: the monthly plan now also has the one-week free trial, as the yearly plan did.

DEMO ACCOUNTS
- Account A: the account in Sign-In Information above.
- Account B: [handle and sign-in for Account B, added in App Store Connect only]
The two accounts share a conversation in which each has sent the other more than 5 messages, so calls and the chat camera are unlocked there.

WHAT CHANGED
- New look and navigation: Home, Chats, Echo, People, Me. Home opens on the next thing to do: who is waiting for a reply, the day's streak, the Echo review cards that are ready.
- Pro Week: a new account (18 or over) starts with seven days of LangX Pro, given at no charge. Nothing is bought, no payment details are asked for, and it ends by itself. It is not the App Store free trial, which is still offered afterwards. Seven days of practice in that week extend it by one more week. The demo accounts are not new, so they will not see it; a new sign-up does.
- Invites: when someone you invited has their first real conversation on LangX, you each get a week of Pro, given the same way. Me > Invite a friend.
- A first message to someone who joined in the last 7 days does not count toward the free plan's 5 new conversations a day (at most 3 a day, adults only).
- Sticker packs, profile frames and app icons: stickers and frames are bought with tokens, which are earned in the app by talking, correcting and reviewing. Tokens cannot be bought with money, sold, transferred or withdrawn. Nine of the 17 app icons need LangX Pro.

COPILOT (BETA, FOR CHOSEN ACCOUNTS)
Copilot is an AI helper in a chat, rolling out to accounts we turn it on for; Account A has it. In the conversation, type a sentence, then tap the blue Copilot button beside the send button for "Check my sentence" or "Say it in [language]"; long-press one of Account B's messages for "Explain"; when the other person's newest message is unanswered and your draft is empty, a "Reply ideas" chip appears above it.
The first use opens a consent sheet that names the provider (Anthropic), says what is sent (the text asked about, with your languages and levels; for Reply ideas the last few messages of that chat) and what is never sent (name, email, photos, voice notes). Nothing reaches the provider before you accept. Answers are labelled "AI · may be wrong", and nothing is sent to the other person unless you send it yourself. Copilot is not available to users under 18, and the messages of a user under 18 are never sent to it.

CALLS (UNCHANGED SINCE 2.9, NEW SCREENS)
Sign in as Account A on the review device, and as Account B on a second device or in a desktop browser at https://app.langx.io (Chrome or Safari; allow microphone and camera). Open the conversation between the two accounts, tap the phone button at the top right and choose Voice call or Video call. The other side rings; on an iPhone the incoming call uses the system call screen (CallKit), also when the app is closed. You can call someone only after they have sent you 5 messages; Settings > Privacy > "Allow calls" turns calls off both ways.
PushKit VoIP pushes are used only to ring an incoming call, and each is reported to CallKit as soon as it arrives. The "voip" and "audio" background modes are for calls only: "audio" keeps the call's sound running when the screen is locked during a call. On the China storefront CallKit and PushKit are not used.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
LangX Pro is an auto-renewable subscription, monthly or yearly; a first subscription to either starts with a one-week free trial where the store offers it. The Plans screen is at Me > Settings (gear) > Subscription > See the plans. The Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA), Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy are linked at the end of the App Store description and on the Plans screen.
```

## Sticker pack gifts — only if `stickerGifts` is on

Giving a sticker pack to a chat partner (`flags.stickerGifts`, #1895) is not
in the note above, because the switch starts off and the note describes what
the reviewer can reach. If it is on for the review, the paragraph below goes
into the note, and the "Tokens cannot be bought … transferred" line above stays
true: a gift moves no tokens.

### Before pasting

- [ ] **Decide whether the switch is on for the review.** The flag is
      server-side, so the build App Review sees can have it on or off. Describe
      the feature to a reviewer who cannot reach it, then switch it on after
      approval, and that is what Guideline 2.3.1 calls a hidden feature. So
      either switch `stickerGifts` on (and `v3Home`, without which the pack
      page cannot be reached) before submitting, or leave this paragraph out
      and send it with the submission that comes after the switch. Do not
      describe a feature the reviewer cannot open.
- [ ] **The order in `docs/decisions.md` holds:** the Terms (§6, §7) are
      published, the per-partner correction cap is merged, and token.langx.io
      says what a gift is.
- [ ] **A demo thread that can give.** A reviewer cannot open gifting on fresh
      accounts. Both people need 50 messages each in the thread, the thread
      needs to be 3 days old, and the giver's account 14 days old. Both demo

      Write the thread from the two accounts in the app, not by a database
      insert, because the gate reads `messageCountBy`. The giver also needs

### The paragraph

**It does not fit as well as everything above.** The note above is already
close to Apple’s 4,000 characters (3,950), and this paragraph is about 1,900 more. If
gifting is on for the review, something has to give — the CALLS section, which

```text
STICKER PACK GIFTS
LangX has an in-app point called tokens. Tokens come from activity in the app (for example sending messages, correcting someone's sentence, recording a pronunciation, finishing a review, keeping a streak) and from small bonuses LangX gives, such as a starting balance and a free hourly gift. They cannot be purchased with money or anything else, have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn, and cannot be sent from one user to another. They are spent only on streak freezes, missed-day repairs and cosmetic items such as sticker packs. They cannot buy the subscription or any of its features.

A user can spend their own earned tokens to give a sticker pack to someone they chat with. The giver pays for a new copy of the pack, and the pack becomes the recipient's. No tokens move between the two users: the recipient's balance does not change. A gift cannot be passed on, returned or refunded. A gift carries no message of its own; it appears in the conversation as a card showing the pack.

Limits:
- Both people must be 18 or older. Users under 18 can neither give nor receive a gift.
- Only between people in an established conversation: each has sent the other at least 50 messages, and the conversation is at least 3 days old. The giver's account must be at least 14 days old.
- One gift sent per week, two received per month.
- No gifts where either person has blocked or reported the other, to or from suspended accounts, or to official accounts.
- Only sticker packs can be given. Frames, titles and streak items cannot.

TO TRY IT
Sign in as Account A and open the conversation with Account B [sign-in for Account B, added in App Store Connect only]. Tap the sticker button next to the message field, open a sticker pack, and tap "Give this pack". Pick Account B, then confirm. The pack appears in Account B's stickers, and a card for the gift appears in the conversation for both.
```

### What the paragraph leans on

Each claim is the code's or the Terms', so a reviewer who tests it finds it
true:

- **Activity and bonuses, never bought.** `packages/shared/src/token.ts`
  (the header comment and `TOKEN_KINDS`): besides activity, the starting
  balance, the hourly gift, the daily pool, referrals and the operator's
  bounty and report rewards. No kind is credited by a payment from the person
  who receives it. **One exception to watch:** `referralSubscription` pays the
  _referrer_ when somebody they invited starts a paid plan. Nobody can buy
  their own tokens that way, and `TOKEN_RULES.referral.subscription` says why
  that is not a purchase. Still, a reviewer who asks "can money ever produce
  tokens" deserves that answer, not "never". The note says "cannot be
  purchased", which is true of every user.
- **Spent only on freezes, repairs and cosmetics, never the subscription.**
  `TOKEN_RULES.sinks`, the `token.ts` header, `docs/decisions.md`. The note
  says "the subscription or any of its features" rather than "no paid
  feature" on purpose: a freeze protects a streak, and a streak can earn a
  gift of Pro, which is the same reason streak items cannot be given.
- **A new copy, no tokens moved, the recipient's balance unchanged.**
  `apps/api/src/modules/tokens/stickerGifts.ts`: one transaction charges the
  giver and grants the pack. Before they confirm, the giver reads
  `stickerGift.honesty`: the pack becomes theirs, cannot be returned or
  refunded, and the tokens are not given back.
- **Cannot be passed on, returned or refunded.** Terms §7, _Gifts between
  users_ (langx/website#288).
- **18 and over at both ends.** `isAdult` on both sides in `stickerGifts.ts`;
  the Terms §7 say the same.
- **50 messages each, 3 days, 14-day giver, 1 a week, 2 a month.**
  `STICKER_GIFT_RULES` in `packages/shared/src/stickerGift.ts`. The caps are
  unique indexes in `apps/api/src/db/indexes.ts`, not counts.
- **Blocks, reports, suspended, official.** The refusals listed in
  `docs/decisions.md` → _A sticker pack can be given to a chat partner, behind
  a switch_.
- **No message with a gift.** There is no free-text field. The row is written
  by the server (`isServerRecord`).
- **Sticker packs only.** `isGiftableCosmetic`.

**Why it holds under 3.1.1.** In-app purchase rules apply to digital items
bought with money. Nothing here is bought with money: the tokens are earned,
and they are not for sale. If tokens or items are ever sold, this paragraph
stops being true and gifting falls under in-app purchase rules. The gifting
entry in `docs/decisions.md` says so too.
